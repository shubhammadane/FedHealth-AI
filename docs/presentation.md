# Presentation Outline — FedHealth-AI

## Slide 1 — Project Title
Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning Framework for Diabetes Hospital Readmission Prediction

## Slide 2 — Problem Statement
Healthcare institutions may face barriers to combining sensitive patient records for collaborative AI.

## Slide 3 — Motivation
Collaborative learning can be useful while reducing the need to centralize raw training data.

## Slide 4 — Centralized Healthcare AI
All training data is moved to a central location.

## Slide 5 — Federated Learning
Clients train locally and share model updates.

## Slide 6 — Proposed Architecture
Five simulated hospital clients, local training, weighted FedAvg, global model.

## Slide 7 — Dataset
UCI Diabetes 130-US Hospitals for Years 1999–2008, dataset ID 296.

## Slide 8 — Data Preprocessing
Imputation, feature selection, encoding, scaling, stratified split.

## Slide 9 — Hospital Client Simulation
Five simulated clients. IID and controlled Non-IID modes.

## Slide 10 — FedAvg
w_global = Σ (n_k / n) w_k

## Slide 11 — Experimental Setup
Compare centralized, local, FedAvg, and FedProx where available.

## Slide 12 — Results
Show actual metrics generated by the experiment.

## Slide 13 — Centralized vs Federated
Compare accuracy, precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC.

## Slide 14 — Explainable AI
Permutation feature importance; interpret as model association, not causality.

## Slide 15 — Privacy
Raw records stay on clients in the simulation; additional privacy mechanisms may be needed.

## Slide 16 — Limitations
Simulated clients, public historical dataset, no clinical validation.

## Slide 17 — Future Work
Secure aggregation, differential privacy, robust FL, multimodal FL, real deployment.

## Slide 18 — Conclusion
FedHealth-AI demonstrates a reproducible research workflow for privacy-aware collaborative healthcare AI.
